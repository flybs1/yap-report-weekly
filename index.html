ASSUMPTIONS & RULES

Why this exists

This is not meant as a put-down or call-out. It is meant as a friendly way to rib each other into better comm discipline.

What is logged

When one player has been transmitting on a frequency for more than 0.5 seconds, any other player (including multiple players) who pushes the transmit button is logged as a stepped-on comm.

If the second player starts transmitting less than 5 seconds from the end of the previous transmission, it is not logged as stepped-on.

What the logger does not consider

  • Role: Element / Flight / Package Lead, GCI
  • Priority: Threat, Targeting, Administration
  • Terrain: Two flights on opposite sides of the map or opposite sides of a mountain will still be logged as stepped on based on PTT time.
